No player qualifies for the 1930 FIFA Men's World Cup edition.

**Reasoning:**

The Ballon d'Or was first awarded in 1956 (to Stanley Matthews), and the UEFA Men's Player of the Year / UEFA Best Player in Europe Award was first presented in 1998 (to Ronaldo). The 1930 World Cup squads were composed entirely of players from the pre-Ballon d'Or era. I need to check whether any player who appeared in a 1930 World Cup squad *ever* won either award, at any point in their career (past or future).

The 1930 World Cup featured 13 nations: Uruguay, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Bolivia, Peru, Paraguay, USA, France, Belgium, Romania, Yugoslavia, and Mexico.

The Ballon d'Or winners from 1956 onward are well-documented. The earliest winners include Stanley Matthews (1956), Alfredo Di Stéfano (1957, 1959), Raymond Kopa (1958), and so on. None of these players were born early enough to have participated in the 1930 World Cup (players active in 1930 would typically have been born between roughly 1900 and 1912). No Ballon d'Or winner was born in that era and remained active enough to win the award starting in 1956.

The UEFA Men's Player of the Year award began in 1998, well after any 1930 player's career.

Therefore, **no player** who was named to any national team squad at the 1930 FIFA World Cup has ever won the Ballon d'Or or the UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award (under any of its names).
