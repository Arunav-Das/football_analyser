**Step 1 — Confirm the 1958 FIFA World Cup was actually held**
The 1958 FIFA World Cup was held in Sweden from 8 June to 29 June 1958. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

**Step 3 — Identify which of those squad players have ever won the Ballon d'Or or UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award**
The Ballon d'Or has been awarded since 1956 by France Football. The UEFA Men's Player of the Year (previously UEFA Best Player in Europe / UEFA Club Footballer of the Year) has been awarded since 1998. Cross-referencing the 1958 squad lists with all-time Ballon d'Or winners:
3. Wikipedia — "Ballon d'Or": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or
4. Wikipedia — "UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Men%27s_Player_of_the_Year_Award

Qualifying players identified:
- **Raymond Kopa** (France squad) — won Ballon d'Or 1958
- **Lev Yashin** (Soviet Union squad) — won Ballon d'Or 1963
- **Bobby Charlton** (England squad) — won Ballon d'Or 1966
- **Josef Masopust** (Czechoslovakia squad) — won Ballon d'Or 1962

No player from the 1958 squads has won the UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award (first awarded 1998, and no 1958 squad player was active at that time).
