The 1934 FIFA World Cup predates both the Ballon d'Or (first awarded 1956) and the UEFA Men's Player of the Year / Best Player in Europe Award (first awarded 1998). To qualify, a player from the 1934 squads would need to have won one of these awards *after* the tournament. I have examined whether any participant in the 1934 World Cup later won a Ballon d'Or or UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award, and none did. The Ballon d'Or inaugural winner was Stanley Matthews in 1956, and no member of any 1934 World Cup squad won a Ballon d'Or or UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award at any point in their career.
