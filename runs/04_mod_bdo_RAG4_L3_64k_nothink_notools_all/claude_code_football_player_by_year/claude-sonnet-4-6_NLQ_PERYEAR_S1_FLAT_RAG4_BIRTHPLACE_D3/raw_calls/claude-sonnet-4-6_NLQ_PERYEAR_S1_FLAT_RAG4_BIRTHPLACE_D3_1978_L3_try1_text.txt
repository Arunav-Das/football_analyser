The 1978 FIFA World Cup was held in Argentina from 1 June to 25 June 1978. It was the 11th edition of the tournament and was not cancelled or abandoned.

**Step 3 — Confirm Ballon d'Or wins for each qualifying player**
The Ballon d'Or is awarded by France Football. Award history is documented as follows:
- Karl-Heinz Rummenigge won the Ballon d'Or in 1980 and 1981.

- Paolo Rossi won the Ballon d'Or in 1982.
None of the three won a UEFA Men's Player of the Year / UEFA Best Player in Europe / UEFA Club Footballer of the Year award, so only the Ballon d'Or is listed for each.
