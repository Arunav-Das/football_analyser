**STEPS AND CITATIONS IN ORDER OF USE**

**Step 1 — Confirm the 1962 FIFA World Cup was actually held**

The 1962 FIFA World Cup was held in Chile from 30 May to 17 June 1962. It was the 7th edition of the tournament and was not cancelled or abandoned.

> **Citation [1]:** FIFA Official Website – "FIFA World Cup Chile 1962", https://www.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/chile1962

---

**Step 2 — Identify all squads named to the 1962 World Cup**

The 1962 World Cup featured 16 nations. Squad lists are documented on Wikipedia's dedicated page for the tournament squads.

> **Citation [2]:** Wikipedia – "1962 FIFA World Cup squads", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_FIFA_World_Cup_squads

The 16 participating nations and their squads were: Brazil, Czechoslovakia, Soviet Union, Yugoslavia, Chile, West Germany, Hungary, Italy, Argentina, Spain, Uruguay, Colombia, Mexico, Bulgaria, England, Switzerland.

---

**Step 3 — Identify Ballon d'Or winners and UEFA Men's Player of the Year winners among squad members**

The Ballon d'Or has been awarded since 1956. The UEFA Club Footballer of the Year / UEFA Best Player in Europe Award has been awarded since 1998. I cross-reference the full historical winners list against the squad rosters:

> **Citation [3]:** Wikipedia – "Ballon d'Or", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or  
> **Citation [4]:** Wikipedia – "UEFA Club Footballer of the Year", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Club_Footballer_of_the_Year  
> **Citation [5]:** Wikipedia – "UEFA Best Player in Europe Award", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Best_Player_in_Europe_Award

Confirmed qualifying players:

**Josef Masopust** – Czechoslovakia squad, 1962. Won Ballon d'Or 1962 (the same year as the World Cup). He was the first Czech winner of the award. ✓

**Lev Yashin** – Soviet Union squad, 1962. Won Ballon d'Or 1963, the only goalkeeper ever to win the award. He played in the 1962 World Cup, where the USSR reached the quarterfinals. ✓

**Gianni Rivera** – Italy squad, 1962. Rivera was 18 years old at the 1962 World Cup and was included in the Italian squad. He won the Ballon d'Or in 1969. ✓

**Florian Albert** – Hungary squad, 1962. Albert played for Hungary at the 1962 World Cup (Hungary reached the quarterfinals). He won the Ballon d'Or in 1967. ✓

**Luis Suárez Miramontes** – Spain squad, 1962. He won the Ballon d'Or in 1960, two years before the World Cup. He was included in Spain's 1962 World Cup squad. ✓

**Dragan Džajić** – Was NOT at 1962 World Cup (too young, born 1946). Excluded.

**Garrincha** – Did NOT win the Ballon d'Or outright (he was runner-up in 1962 and 1963 but never won). UEFA awards did not exist yet. Excluded.

**Pelé** – Pelé was part of Brazil's 1962 squad but was injured early and played only two matches. Pelé never officially won the Ballon d'Or (he was excluded from eligibility as a non-European until the award was opened up globally much later; he received a special Ballon d'Or in 2013 but this is an honorary award, not a competitive edition win). I will exclude him as the honorary award is not the same as a competitive Ballon d'Or win under the standard criteria. *(Note: Some sources treat the 2013 special prize differently; to be conservative, Pelé is excluded.)*

**Dino Zoff** – Was NOT in the Italian 1962 squad (he was 20 and not yet called up for the 1962 World Cup). Italy's 1962 goalkeepers were Buffon (Lorenzo Buffon), not Dino Zoff. Excluded.

No UEFA Men's Player of the Year / UEFA Club Footballer of the Year award winners were part of the 1962 World Cup squads, as that award was first given in 1998 and the relevant players from 1962 had already retired.
