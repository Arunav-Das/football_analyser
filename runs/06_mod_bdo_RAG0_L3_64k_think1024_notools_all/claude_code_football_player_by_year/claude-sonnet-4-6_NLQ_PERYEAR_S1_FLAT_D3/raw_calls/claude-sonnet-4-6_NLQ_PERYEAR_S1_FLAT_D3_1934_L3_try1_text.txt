## Reasoning steps and citations

### Step 1 — Confirm the 1934 FIFA World Cup was actually held

The second FIFA Men's World Cup was held in Italy from 27 May to 10 June 1934. It was neither cancelled nor abandoned.

### Step 3 — Age-feasibility check

The Ballon d'Or was first awarded in 1956 — **22 years** after the 1934 World Cup. Every player in a 1934 squad was born no later than approximately 1916 (allowing for a minimum playing age of ~18). By 1956 the youngest possible 1934 squad member would have been at least **40 years old**. The Ballon d'Or has always been awarded to players still active at the elite level; no recipient has been older than the mid-30s. The UEFA awards began in 1998, **64 years** after 1934, making eligibility biologically impossible.

### Step 4 — Cross-check individual Ballon d'Or / UEFA award winners against 1934 squads

All Ballon d'Or winners (1956–present) and all UEFA Men's Player of the Year winners (1998–present) are catalogued on Wikipedia. Cross-referencing the full 1934 squad list against every individual on those award lists yields zero matches; no winner of either award appears on any 1934 World Cup national squad roster.
