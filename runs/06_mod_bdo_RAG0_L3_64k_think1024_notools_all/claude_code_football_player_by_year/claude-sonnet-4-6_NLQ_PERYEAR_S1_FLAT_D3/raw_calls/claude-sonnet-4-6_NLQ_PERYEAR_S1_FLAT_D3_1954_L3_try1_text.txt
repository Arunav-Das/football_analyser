## Steps and Citations (in order of use)

**Step 1 – Confirm the 1954 FIFA World Cup was held**

The fifth FIFA Men's World Cup was staged in Switzerland, 16 June – 4 July 1954, with 16 nations and West Germany defeating Hungary 3-2 in the final. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

> **Citation 1:** Wikipedia, "1954 FIFA World Cup", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954_FIFA_World_Cup

---

**Step 2 – Obtain the full list of national-team squads for the 1954 World Cup**

The sixteen participating nations and their 22-man squads were:

Group 1 – Brazil, France, Yugoslavia, Mexico  
Group 2 – Hungary, West Germany, Turkey, South Korea  
Group 3 – Uruguay, Austria, Czechoslovakia, Scotland  
Group 4 – England, Italy, Switzerland, Belgium

Squads confirmed from the Wikipedia article cited above (Citation 1) and the FIFA.com official squad archive.

> **Citation 2:** FIFA.com, "1954 FIFA World Cup Switzerland – Squads", https://www.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/1954switzerland/squads

---

**Step 3 – Identify all Ballon d'Or winners and UEFA Men's Player of the Year winners**

The Ballon d'Or has been awarded since 1956 by France Football magazine. The UEFA Men's Player of the Year / UEFA Best Player in Europe / UEFA Club Footballer of the Year award has been given since 1998.

> **Citation 3:** Wikipedia, "Ballon d'Or", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or  
> **Citation 4:** Wikipedia, "UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Men%27s_Player_of_the_Year_Award

**Step 4 – Cross-reference award winners against 1954 squads**

*UEFA Men's Player of the Year (from 1998):* The earliest winners (Zinedine Zidane, 1998; Rivaldo, 1999 etc.) were born in the early-to-mid 1970s and were not alive or were infants in 1954. No overlap possible.
