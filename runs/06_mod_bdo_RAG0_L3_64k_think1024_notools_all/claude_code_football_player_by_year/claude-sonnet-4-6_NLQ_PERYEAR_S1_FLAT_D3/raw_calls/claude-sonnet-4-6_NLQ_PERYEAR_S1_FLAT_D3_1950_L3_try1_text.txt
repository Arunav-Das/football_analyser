**Steps and Citations in order of use:**

**Step 1 — Confirm the 1950 FIFA World Cup was actually held**
The fourth FIFA Men's World Cup was staged in Brazil from 24 June to 16 July 1950. It was the first post-war edition, held after the 1942 and 1946 tournaments were cancelled.
1. Wikipedia, "1950 FIFA World Cup" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_FIFA_World_Cup

**Step 2 — Establish the list of squads / players named to national team squads**
Thirteen nations participated (India withdrew without playing). The squads are listed on the Wikipedia article for the tournament and its dedicated squad-list article.
2. Wikipedia, "1950 FIFA World Cup squads" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1950_FIFA_World_Cup_squads

Key squad facts relied upon:
- England participated in their first-ever World Cup in 1950; **Stanley Matthews** (born 1 February 1915) was selected in the England squad.
- Other notable squads: Brazil, Uruguay, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Yugoslavia, Switzerland, Chile, USA, Mexico, Paraguay, Bolivia.

**Step 3 — Establish which players from those squads have won the Ballon d'Or or UEFA Men's Player of the Year (any name)**

The Ballon d'Or was first awarded in 1956 (by France Football). The UEFA Club Footballer of the Year / UEFA Best Player in Europe / UEFA Men's Player of the Year has been awarded since 1998. Because 1950 World Cup players would have been retired by 1998, only the Ballon d'Or is relevant.

Screening the squads:
- **Stanley Matthews** (England) — won the inaugural **Ballon d'Or in 1956** at age 41, while still playing for Blackpool. He is the oldest recipient of the award. ✓ Qualifies.
- Juan Schiaffino (Uruguay) — finished 2nd in the 1954 Ballon d'Or vote but never won it. ✗
- Gunnar Nordahl (Sweden) — received votes but never won the Ballon d'Or. ✗
- Zizinho, Ademir, Ghiggia, Obdulio Varela, and others from the squads — none won the Ballon d'Or or UEFA award at any point in their careers. ✗
- Alfredo Di Stéfano (multiple Ballon d'Or wins) — was not in a 1950 World Cup squad; Argentina withdrew and Colombia (which he represented in 1949–1952) did not participate. ✗

3. Wikipedia, "Ballon d'Or" (history and list of winners) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or
4. Wikipedia, "Stanley Matthews" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Matthews

**Step 4 — Confirm Stanley Matthews's birthplace**
Stanley Matthews was born on 1 February 1915 in **Hanley**, one of the six towns that make up the City of Stoke-on-Trent, Staffordshire, England.
5. Wikipedia, "Stanley Matthews" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Matthews

**Step 5 — Population of Hanley, Stoke-on-Trent**
Hanley is the city centre and principal commercial district of Stoke-on-Trent; it does not carry a separate unitary census figure but is often cited at approximately 78,000 for the Hanley-defined area. The City of Stoke-on-Trent as a whole had a population of approximately 270,000–275,000 at the 2021 England and Wales Census.
