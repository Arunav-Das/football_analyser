No player qualifies. After exhaustively cross-referencing the squads of all 15 participating nations at the 1938 FIFA World Cup against every Ballon d'Or winner (first awarded 1956) and every UEFA Men's Player of the Year / Best Player in Europe winner (first awarded 1998), no player named to a 1938 World Cup squad has ever won either award.

**Reasoning:** The Ballon d'Or was not established until 1956. Even the youngest realistic squad members in 1938 (e.g., born ~1920, age ~18) would have been approximately 36 years old in 1956 — well past the age at which players typically win the award — and their careers were further disrupted by World War II (1939–1945). The UEFA Men's Player of the Year family of awards did not begin until 1998, by which time all 1938 World Cup participants had long since retired. No overlap exists.
