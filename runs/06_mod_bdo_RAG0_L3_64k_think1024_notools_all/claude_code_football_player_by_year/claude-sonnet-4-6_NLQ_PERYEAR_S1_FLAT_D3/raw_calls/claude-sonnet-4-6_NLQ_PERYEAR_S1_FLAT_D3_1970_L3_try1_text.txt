## Steps and Citations (in order of use)

**Step 1 — Confirm the 1970 FIFA Men's World Cup was actually held**

The 9th FIFA Men's World Cup was held in Mexico from 31 May to 21 June 1970. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

> **Citation 1:** Wikipedia, "1970 FIFA World Cup" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_FIFA_World_Cup

---

**Step 2 — Obtain the full list of players named to national team squads for the 1970 World Cup**

Sixteen nations participated. Squad lists (22 players each, in some cases slightly fewer) are documented on Wikipedia's dedicated squad pages and on the official FIFA technical report for the tournament.

> **Citation 2a:** Wikipedia, "1970 FIFA World Cup squads" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1970_FIFA_World_Cup_squads

> **Citation 2b:** FIFA Technical Study Group Report 1970 (reproduced/referenced on FIFA.com) — https://www.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/1970mexico

---

**Step 3 — Identify which Ballon d'Or / UEFA Men's Player of the Year winners appeared in those squads**

The Ballon d'Or (awarded by France Football) was restricted to European players up to and including the 1994 edition, then opened globally from 1995. The UEFA Men's Player of the Year award (under various names: UEFA Club Footballer of the Year from 1998, UEFA Best Player in Europe Award from 2011) began only in 1998; all winners of the UEFA award were born in the 1970s or later and therefore could not have been in a 1970 World Cup squad. Accordingly, only Ballon d'Or winners are relevant here.
