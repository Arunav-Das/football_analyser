### Step 1 — Confirm the 1966 FIFA Men's World Cup was actually held

The eighth FIFA Men's World Cup was held in England from 11 to 30 July 1966. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

**Citation [1]:** FIFA Official Website – "1966 FIFA World Cup England™"
https://www.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/1966england

---

### Step 2 — Establish all 16 participating nations and their full 22-man squads

The 16 nations were drawn into four groups:
- Group 1: England, Uruguay, Mexico, France
- Group 2: West Germany, Argentina, Spain, Switzerland
- Group 3: Brazil, Portugal, Hungary, Bulgaria
- Group 4: Soviet Union, Italy, Chile, North Korea

Full squad lists (22 players per team, 352 players total) are documented in the Wikipedia article on the tournament, which aggregates the official FIFA squad sheets.

### Step 5 — Cross-reference winners against squad members, eliminating non-qualifiers

Going through each Ballon d'Or winner above:

- **Stanley Matthews (1956):** Born 1915; at 51 years old in 1966 he was not in England's squad. ✗
- **Alfredo Di Stéfano (1957, 1959):** Born 1926; retired from international football. Argentina did not select him for 1966. ✗
- **Raymond Kopa (1958):** Born 1931; France was at the 1966 World Cup but Kopa had retired from international duty by 1962. Not in squad. ✗
- **Luis Suárez Miramontes (1960):** Born 1935; Spain was in Group 2 of 1966 World Cup. Suárez was 30–31 and was Spain's creative spine at Inter Milan. He was in Spain's 1966 squad. ✓
- **Omar Sívori (1961):** Born 1935; Argentina was at 1966 but Sívori had a falling-out with the Argentine FA and retired from international football around 1961–62 after the dispute. Not in Argentina's 1966 squad. ✗
- **Josef Masopust (1962):** Czechoslovakia did not qualify for the 1966 World Cup. ✗
- **Lev Yashin (1963):** Born 1929; Soviet Union reached the semi-finals in 1966. Yashin played multiple matches and was a key figure. ✓
- **Denis Law (1964):** Scotland did not qualify for the 1966 World Cup. ✗
- **Eusébio (1965):** Born 1942; Portugal made their World Cup debut in 1966 and reached the semi-finals. Eusébio was top scorer (9 goals). ✓
- **Bobby Charlton (1966):** Born 1937; England won the 1966 World Cup. Charlton was a key midfielder. ✓
- **Florian Albert (1967):** Born 1941; Hungary was in Group 3 at 1966. Albert was their star centre-forward. ✓
- **George Best (1968):** Northern Ireland did not qualify for 1966. ✗
- **Gianni Rivera (1969):** Born 1944; Italy was in Group 4 at 1966. Rivera was 22 and an established AC Milan player; he was in Italy's squad. ✓
- **Gerd Müller (1970):** Born 1945; Müller made his West Germany international debut in October 1966, after the World Cup ended. He was not named to the 1966 World Cup squad. ✗
- **Johan Cruyff (1971, 1973, 1974):** Netherlands did not qualify for 1966. ✗
- **Franz Beckenbauer (1972, 1976):** Born 1945; West Germany reached the final in 1966. Beckenbauer, aged 20, was one of the tournament's standout performers. ✓
