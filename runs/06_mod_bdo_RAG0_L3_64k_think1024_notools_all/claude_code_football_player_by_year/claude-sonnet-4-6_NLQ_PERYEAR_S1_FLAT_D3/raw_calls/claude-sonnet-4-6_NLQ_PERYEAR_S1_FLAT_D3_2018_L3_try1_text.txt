## Steps and Citations

**Step 1 — Confirming the 2018 FIFA World Cup was held in the stated calendar year**

The 2018 FIFA World Cup was held in Russia from 14 June to 15 July 2018. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

**Step 2 — Obtaining the full list of players named to national team squads for the 2018 World Cup**

All 32 participating nations submitted 23-man squads. Players confirmed as named to squads and relevant to this search include Lionel Messi (Argentina), Cristiano Ronaldo (Portugal), Luka Modrić (Croatia), Andrés Iniesta (Spain), Robert Lewandowski (Poland), Neymar (Brazil), Kylian Mbappé (France), Antoine Griezmann (France), Harry Kane (England), Kevin De Bruyne (Belgium), and hundreds of others.

**Step 3 — Identifying which of those players have ever won the Ballon d'Or**

The Ballon d'Or is awarded annually (with a gap in 2020 due to COVID-19). Winners relevant to players who appeared in the 2018 World Cup squads:

- **Lionel Messi**: 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, 2023
- **Cristiano Ronaldo**: 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017
- **Luka Modrić**: 2018 (awarded in December 2018, after the World Cup concluded in July — still qualifies because the question is career-independent of timing)
- **Karim Benzema**: 2022 — Benzema was famously excluded from France's national team from 2015 to 2021 and was **not** named to France's 2018 World Cup squad; he does not qualify.
- **Kylian Mbappé**: Through my knowledge cutoff (August 2025), Mbappé has **not** won the Ballon d'Or (Rodri won 2024); excluded.
- All other 2018 squad members reviewed (Neymar, Griezmann, Kane, De Bruyne, Hazard, Suárez, etc.) have not won the Ballon d'Or.

> [3] Wikipedia, "Ballon d'Or" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or
