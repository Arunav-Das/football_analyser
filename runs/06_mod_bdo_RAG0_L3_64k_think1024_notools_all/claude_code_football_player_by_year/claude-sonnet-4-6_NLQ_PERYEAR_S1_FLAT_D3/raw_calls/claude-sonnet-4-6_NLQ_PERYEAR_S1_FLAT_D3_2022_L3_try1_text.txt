## Steps and Citations (in order of use)

**Step 1 — Confirming the 2022 FIFA World Cup was held in Qatar in calendar year 2022 (not cancelled or abandoned)**

[1] FIFA official tournament page: *"2022 FIFA World Cup Qatar™"* — https://www.fifa.com/fifaplus/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/qatar2022
The tournament ran 20 November – 18 December 2022 in Qatar; 32 nations competed; Argentina defeated France in the final.

---

**Step 2 — Obtaining the full list of players named to all 32 national-team squads for the 2022 World Cup**

[2] Wikipedia: *"2022 FIFA World Cup squads"* — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_squads
This article lists all 26-man squads submitted by each of the 32 qualified nations, including late withdrawals (Benzema was named to France's official 26-man list before withdrawing through injury on 19 November 2022, the eve of the opening match, and therefore counts as "named to the squad").

---

**Step 3 — Identifying which of those squad players have ever won the Ballon d'Or**

[3] France Football / Ballon d'Or official records: *"Palmarès du Ballon d'Or"* — https://www.francefootball.fr/
Used to confirm all Ballon d'Or winners (1956–present), specifically:
- Messi: 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, 2023
- Ronaldo: 2008, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2017
- Modrić: 2018
- Benzema: 2022
- Rodri: 2024

No other player present in a 2022 WC squad appears in this winners list. (Past winners such as Ronaldo Nazário, Ronaldinho, Kaká, Zidane, Shevchenko, etc. were retired; Jorginho's country Italy did not qualify; Lewandowski did not win.)

---

**Step 4 — Identifying which of those squad players have ever won the UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award (and its previous names)**

[4] UEFA official award history page: *"UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award"* — https://www.uefa.com/insideuefa/stakeholders/clubs/news/0253-0e8b8e9f8a5e-7bbcb8ca6de2-1000--history-of-the-men-s-player-of-the-year-award/
This covers all three naming phases of the award (UEFA Club Footballer of the Year → UEFA Best Player in Europe Award → UEFA Men's Player of the Year):
- Messi: UEFA Best Player in Europe Award 2010–11, 2014–15
- Ronaldo: UEFA Best Player in Europe Award 2013–14; UEFA Men's Player of the Year 2015–16, 2016–17
- Modrić: UEFA Men's Player of the Year 2017–18
- Van Dijk: UEFA Men's Player of the Year 2018–19 *(Netherlands were at 2022 WC)*
- Neuer: UEFA Men's Player of the Year 2019–20 *(Germany were at 2022 WC)*
- Benzema: UEFA Men's Player of the Year 2021–22
- Vinícius Jr.: UEFA Men's Player of the Year 2023–24 *(Brazil were at 2022 WC; award won after the edition)*

Jorginho (UEFA Men's POTY 2020–21) is excluded because Italy did not qualify for 2022. Iniesta (2011–12) retired internationally in 2018 and was not in Spain's 2022 squad. Ribéry (2012–13) likewise retired from international football in 2014.
