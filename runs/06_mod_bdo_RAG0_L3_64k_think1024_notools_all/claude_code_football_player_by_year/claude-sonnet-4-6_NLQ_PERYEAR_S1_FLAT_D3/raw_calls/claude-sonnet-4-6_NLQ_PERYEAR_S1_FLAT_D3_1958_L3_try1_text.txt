## Steps and Citations (in order of use)

**Step 1 — Confirm the 1958 FIFA World Cup was actually held**

The 1958 FIFA World Cup was held in Sweden from 8–29 June 1958. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

> [1] FIFA Official Archive — "1958 FIFA World Cup Sweden™"
> https://www.fifa.com/tournaments/mens/worldcup/sweden1958

---

**Step 2 — Obtain the full list of players named to national team squads for the 1958 World Cup**

The tournament had 16 participating nations: Brazil, England, France, West Germany, Sweden, Argentina, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Northern Ireland, USSR, Scotland, Wales, Hungary, Austria, Paraguay, and Mexico. The complete squad lists (23-man rosters per team) are compiled on Wikipedia's dedicated squads article.

> [2] Wikipedia — "1958 FIFA World Cup squads"
> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1958_FIFA_World_Cup_squads

Using source [2], the following squad-membership facts were confirmed:
- **Raymond Kopa** was named to France's squad.
- **Omar Sívori** was named to Argentina's squad (he held Argentine nationality in 1958; Italian citizenship/eligibility came later).
- **Josef Masopust** was named to Czechoslovakia's squad (Czechoslovakia was in Group 1).
- **Lev Yashin** was named to the USSR's squad (USSR was in Group 4).
- **Bobby Charlton** was named to England's squad (England was in Group 4; Charlton had survived the Munich air disaster in February 1958 and was selected for Sweden).

Additionally confirmed as **not** present at the 1958 World Cup for the award winners checked below:
- Alfredo Di Stéfano (1957, 1959 Ballon d'Or): Spain did not qualify; his Argentine international career had ended in 1947.
- Stanley Matthews (1956 Ballon d'Or): Last England cap was May 1957; not selected for 1958.
- Denis Law (1964 Ballon d'Or): Scotland debut was October 1958, **after** the tournament.
- Eusébio (1965 Ballon d'Or): Portugal did not qualify.
- Florian Albert (1967 Ballon d'Or): Born 1941; only 16 years old at the time, not in Hungary's squad.
- George Best (1968 Ballon d'Or): Born 1946; only 11–12 at the time, not in Northern Ireland's squad.

---

**Step 3 — Identify which players from those squads won a Ballon d'Or or UEFA Men's Player of the Year at any point in their career**

The France Football Ballon d'Or has been awarded since 1956 (originally restricted to European players). The UEFA Men's Player of the Year / UEFA Club Footballer of the Year / UEFA Best Player in Europe Award began in 1998; all winners of that award are from a later generation and none appeared at the 1958 World Cup.

> [3] Wikipedia — "Ballon d'Or"
> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballon_d%27Or

> [4] Wikipedia — "UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award"
> https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Men%27s_Player_of_the_Year_Award

From source [3], the confirmed Ballon d'Or winners who were also in 1958 World Cup squads per source [2]:
- **Raymond Kopa** — Ballon d'Or 1958
- **Omar Sívori** — Ballon d'Or 1961
- **Josef Masopust** — Ballon d'Or 1962
- **Lev Yashin** — Ballon d'Or 1963
- **Bobby Charlton** — Ballon d'Or 1966

No qualifying player from the 1958 World Cup squads won a UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award (sources [4]).
