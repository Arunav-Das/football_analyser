   - Gianni Rivera: winner 1969 (Italy squad)
   - Gerd Müller: winner 1970 (West Germany squad)
   - Johan Cruyff: winner 1971, 1973, 1974 (Netherlands squad)
   - Franz Beckenbauer: winner 1972, 1976 (West Germany squad)

4. Wikipedia, "UEFA Men's Player of the Year Award," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UEFA_Men%27s_Player_of_the_Year_Award — establishes that this award (under all its names: UEFA Club Footballer of the Year, UEFA Best Player in Europe) was first awarded for the 1997–98 season. All winners from 1998 onward were active players born no earlier than the late 1960s and thus were not in any 1974 World Cup squad. No additional qualifiers arise from this award.
