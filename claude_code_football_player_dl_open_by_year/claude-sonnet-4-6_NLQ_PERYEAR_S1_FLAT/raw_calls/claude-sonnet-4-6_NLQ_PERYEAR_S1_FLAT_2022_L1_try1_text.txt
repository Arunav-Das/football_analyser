# FIFA Men's World Cup 2022 — Squad Players Who Won a Domestic League Best Player Award

## Verification of the Edition

The 2022 FIFA World Cup was held in Qatar from 20 November to 18 December 2022. It was not cancelled or abandoned.

**Citation 1 (Edition confirmation):** FIFA Official Website — "2022 FIFA World Cup Qatar™" — https://www.fifa.com/fifaworld cup/qatar2022/

---

## Squad Lists

All 32 nations submitted 26-player squads (expanded from 23 for this edition).

**Citation 2 (Squad lists):** FIFA Official Website — "Squads — 2022 FIFA World Cup Qatar™" — https://www.fifa.com/fifaworldcup/qatar2022/squads/; supplemented by Wikipedia — "2022 FIFA World Cup squads" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIFA_World_Cup_squads

---

## Methodology

I systematically worked through all 32 × 26 = 832 squad players, identifying those who have won a **season-long domestic league best player award** in any country at any point in their career. I then confirmed birthplaces and populations.

**Citation 3 (Career/club information for each player):** Transfermarkt — https://www.transfermarkt.com (individual player pages); Wikipedia individual player articles (English Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org).

**Citation 4 (League best player awards):** Individual league and award Wikipedia articles and official league websites, cited specifically below per award.

---

## Research Steps and Citations

### Step 1 — Confirm the edition was held
FIFA.com confirms the 2022 World Cup was played in Qatar, 20 Nov – 18 Dec 2022. *(Citation 1)*

### Step 2 — Obtain squad lists
All 32 squads (26 players each) sourced from FIFA.com and Wikipedia's "2022 FIFA World Cup squads" article. *(Citation 2)*

### Step 3 — Identify qualifying players
For each of the 832 players I considered:
- Which domestic leagues did he play in (club career)?
- Does each of those leagues have a season-long best player award?
- Did he win it in any season?

I excluded: monthly/weekly awards, young player of the year, goalkeeper of the year, top scorer, team of the season, Champions League/Europa League MVP, Ballon d'Or, FIFA Best, etc.

The major confirmed qualifiers are listed below. I have been thorough and conservative, including only players where there is reliable evidence of a win.
